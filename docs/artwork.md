# Card artwork

The app bundles the art-only crops supplied by Scryfall for offline use in 1v1.
No card frames or rules text are included. Images are retained at their source
resolution without upscaling; all selected printings are marked `highres_image`
by Scryfall. These are card-scan art crops (571–626 pixels wide), not original
painting masters. A colour blend and dark overlay keep the life totals readable.

Card artwork © Wizards of the Coast and the credited artists. These assets are
excluded from the source code's MIT licence. This fan project is not endorsed by
Wizards of the Coast. Each artist is also credited in the in-app picker.
